Subscriptions & tokens

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Flore offers premium plans (Pro, Max and King) and tokens, sold through Google Play.

  • Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period until you cancel.
  • You can cancel at any time in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions. You keep premium until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.
  • Tokens are used inside the app, have no cash value and cannot be transferred.
