Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains how Flore ("we", "the app"), operated by Joyal Sabu, collects, uses and shares your information.

Information we collect

  • Account: your Google account email and ID when you sign in with Google.
  • Profile: name or username, date of birth (to confirm you are 18+), gender, preferences, bio and other answers you add.
  • Photos and avatar: profile photos you upload and the avatar you create. Real photos stay hidden from other members until your connection with a match unlocks them.
  • Verification selfie: an optional selfie used only to give you a verified badge. It is never shown to other members.
  • Messages: chat messages and voice notes you send to matches, so they can be delivered and so we can act on reports.
  • Approximate location: coarse location, converted to a city, used to show nearby members and approximate distance. We never collect precise GPS location.
  • Purchases: subscription and token purchase records from Google Play. We never receive your card details.
  • Device and diagnostics: push notification token, app version, crash reports.
  • Safety: blocks, reports and moderation actions.

How we use it

  • To create your account and show your profile to other adult members.
  • To suggest matches, deliver messages and send notifications.
  • To process purchases and unlock premium features.
  • To keep members safe: verification, moderation, and preventing fraud, spam and underage use.
  • To fix crashes and improve the app.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Other members see the profile information you choose to show. We use these service providers to run the app:

  • Supabase (database, file storage, sign-in)
  • Google Firebase (push notifications, Crashlytics crash reports)
  • Google Play (payments)

We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access is restricted by database security rules.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile > ⋮ menu > Delete account) or by following these steps. Your profile, photos, avatar, messages and selfie are deleted within 30 days. Purchase records may be kept longer where the law requires it.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, withdraw location permission and turn off notifications. Email us to make a request.

Children

Flore is only for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18 and we remove such accounts when we find them. See our Child Safety Standards.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the date above.
