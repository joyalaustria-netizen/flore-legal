Delete your Flore account

Last updated: 11 November 2026

How to delete your account in Flore, the dating app published by Joyal Sabu.

In the app

  1. Open Flore and go to your Profile.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Email joyalaustria@gmail.com from the Google account you use with Flore, with the subject "Delete my Flore account". We delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted

Your profile, photos, avatar, verification selfie, matches, messages, voice notes and location are deleted within 30 days.

What may be kept

Purchase records may be kept for up to 8 years for tax and legal reasons. Records of serious safety violations may be kept to stop banned members from returning.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions.
