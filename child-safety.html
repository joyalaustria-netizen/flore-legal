Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Flore has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming and any sexualisation of minors.

Our standards

  • Flore is for adults 18+ only. Every member confirms their date of birth at sign-up, and accounts that appear to belong to minors are removed.
  • Any content or behaviour that sexualises or endangers minors is banned, and the accounts involved are permanently removed.
  • Members can report any profile or message in the app (⋮ menu > Report). Our moderation team reviews every report.
  • We report apparent CSAM to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and local law enforcement, and we preserve evidence as the law requires.
  • We comply with child safety laws in every country where Flore is available.
